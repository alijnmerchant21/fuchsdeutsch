# B1 = Fuchsbau-WG Season 3: "Entscheidungen"

Authoring notes for content/b1/. Follows the order, themes and grammar of Netzwerk neu B1 (12 chapters). All texts, dialogues and exercises are our own.

## Where we are
One year after A2 (A2 ended with Kenji opening his restaurant "Fuchs & Yuzu" on the ground floor of the Fuchsbau building, Leipzig-Plagwitz, Karl-Heine-Straße).
- Kenji (30, Osaka, chef) now lives in the small flat behind his restaurant downstairs. He is still part of the WG family and eats upstairs often. Restaurant is busy, he works too much.
- His old room goes to **Sofia** (27, São Paulo, UX designer at a Leipzig software startup, new to Germany, speaks good but imperfect German, lively, curious, loves dancing and plants).
- **Nele** (24, Leipzig) finished her studies (Kulturwissenschaften) and starts her first real job at a Leipzig cultural foundation; she gets involved in the neighborhood.
- **Amara** (32, Accra) is now Stationsleiterin (ward manager) in the hospital; in K4 she gets an offer from a hospital in Hamburg.
- **Felix** (27, Vienna, musician) and his band "Die Fuchsbande" play more and more; girlfriend **Lina** (27, Leipzig, primary school teacher).
- **Herr Lehmann** (76, Saxon neighbor, has lived in the house since 1968, knows its history).
- **Herr Vogt** (50, Munich, property manager for the new owner "Elbstein Immobilien GmbH", formal, not a villain, just business).

## Season arc
K1 Sofia arrives; WG trip to the Black Forest. K2 tech trouble, repair café, complaint about Kenji's broken oven, an ad. K3 letter: the building has been sold; Herr Lehmann tells its history. K4 Amara's Hamburg offer, interview. K5 courtyard garden, environment. K6 plans for the future, Felix's band. K7 Nele and Felix argue (friendship, conflict). K8 Kenji overworks, hospital, health. K9 Felix's video goes viral, theater evening. K10 tenants organize, neighborhood group (Passiv). K11 Leipzig, petition to the city. K12 money: a housing cooperative buys the building, Amara decides to stay, finale party.

## Timeline K4 to K12 (follow it so chapters fit together)
- K4 Arbeitswelt (December 2026): a hospital in Hamburg offers Amara a job as Pflegedienstleitung (more money, bigger team). She has a video interview. She must decide by the end of March.
- K5 Umweltfreundlich? (January 2027): Sofia and Nele plan a courtyard garden for spring and a foodsharing shelf with leftovers from Kenji's restaurant. Winter weather. Herr Vogt says the courtyard will become parking spaces after the renovation.
- K6 Blick nach vorn (February 2027): plans and resolutions. Felix's band gets an offer to play at a summer festival. Amara still undecided. Nele founds the tenant group "Fuchsbau bleibt!" with Herr Lehmann.
- K7 Zwischenmenschliches (March 2027): Nele and Felix argue: Felix missed two tenant meetings because of band rehearsals. They talk it out. Lina and Sofia mediate. Amara asks Hamburg for more time.
- K8 Rund um Körper und Geist (April 2027): Kenji overworks, collapses in the restaurant kitchen, two days in hospital (Amara's hospital). Rules in the hospital. He learns to take breaks and hires a sous-chef.
- K9 Kunststücke (May 2027): Felix writes the song "Unser Haus" about the Fuchsbau; the video goes viral. Theater evening: Lina's school class plays a piece about the neighborhood.
- K10 Miteinander (June 2027): the tenants organize: Mieterverein, a neighborhood meeting, a plan. Lots of passive voice: what is being done, what was decided.
- K11 Stadt, Land, Fluss (July 2027): life in Leipzig, a petition to the city council, a hearing in the Neues Rathaus. Sofia writes to different recipients (city, press, friends).
- K12 Geld regiert die Welt? (August 2027): bank talks; the tenants found a housing cooperative (Wohnungsgenossenschaft "Fuchsbau eG") with a loan and shares; Elbstein sells to the cooperative. Amara turns down Hamburg and stays (she is offered the Leipzig ward management plus training). Summer party in the courtyard, one year after Sofia arrived.
